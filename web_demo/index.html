HOW IT WORKS

Method and scope

This project demonstrates a working end-to-end path. It does not claim production or regulatory readiness.

01

Structured triage

Stage one returns only Pydantic-constrained type and priority. Confidence comes from token probabilities, not self-report.

02

Deterministic evidence selection

Relevant regulatory sources and internal procedures are selected from the email while retaining evidence IDs.

03

Constrained drafting

Stage two must not invent timing, refunds, outcomes, channels, forms or actions that have already happened.

04

Human final decision

The output is always a draft. A reviewer may accept, edit or discard it; the system itself cannot send.

ASSIGNMENT CLAIM

Suitable to demonstrate feasibility

Five frozen cases complete generation, scoring and human review. Tests cover the schema contract, injection and refusal boundaries.

NOT CLAIMED

Not production-ready

The sample cannot estimate a real error rate, internal procedures are synthetic, and deployment, access control, monitoring and regulatory approval are out of scope.
